## 1. 实现摘要

The isolated `WorkspaceWatchCoordinator` now owns one logical `main` lifecycle. It proves a local main `ResourceRef` through the T-10 ResourceIO root/target seam before closing an old lifecycle, shares one physical watcher and one baseline per repair cycle across logical consumers, and exposes only normalized relative observations plus the four allowed health states.

Mounted ResourceRefs are rejected before a lifecycle change. Root replacement or unavailable proof clears cached facts, closes the watcher, and reaches `FAILED`; scoped retry starts a new repair cycle. A safe shared cutover descriptor describes T-12's required stop/prove/start order without assigning production ownership or modifying `desktop/main.cjs`.

**D-T11-01 successor correction (round 1 / 3):** after zero logical consumers, the next subscriber receives `RECONCILING`, revalidates root/scope before reopening, and receives exactly one fresh baseline rather than cached facts or stale `HEALTHY`. The coordinator now serializes the baseline and every watcher callback through its single gate. It buffers baseline observations until completion proof succeeds, and each ordinary watcher event revalidates before it can update cache or publish. Replaced, unavailable, or changed-scope proof clears facts, closes the watcher, and publishes no normal event.
