## 1. 实现摘要

`b9315d4b` remains rejected. This round tightens File History only, without changing ResourceIO, Workspace runtime, Engine, Knowledge, Desktop, or production assembly.

- History relative paths and opaque private-store keys reject Windows drive prefixes, NUL, and all Unicode control characters.
- The injected History reader now receives `{ maxBytes: 5 MiB }` before content materialization. It can return `truncated`; an oversized or malformed response is not stored, and the store independently rejects oversize buffers.
- Shared baseline capture uses one coalesced drain timer, a 128-item queue turnover batch, and at most four concurrent source reads. Retention runs at initialization, after a completed drain, on the lifecycle interval, or retry, never per snapshot.
- Health derives from unresolved shared repair cycles, failed capture requests, and retention failure. A successful unrelated capture cannot clear another unresolved failure.
- Delete, rename, and completed baseline-diff reconciliation invalidate pending, failed, and in-flight old-path captures before they can write a stale timeline.
- Failed delete, rename, and reconciliation store work now retains retry state. When a failed rename is the predecessor, a later delete or rename is queued without writing early; retries stop at the first unresolved operation and replay the queue in observation order. A fresh capture removes superseded source-path chains transitively without discarding a prior migration into that capture path.
- `activateMain`, `retryMainHistory`, and `close` run through one lifecycle queue with a generation guard.
- Baseline deletion reconciliation consumes existing `workspace.baseline` and `workspace.health`/`repairCycle` signals only after `HEALTHY` completes the repair cycle; no shared contract changed.
